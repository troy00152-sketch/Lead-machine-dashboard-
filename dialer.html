Live coach — script

Opener

"Hi, this is Irish with Direct Property Buyers. I'm calling about your property — we buy houses in the area for cash, as-is. Is now an okay time for two quick questions?"

Qualify

  • "Is the property still yours, and are you the one who'd make the decision to sell?"
  • "What's the situation with it — are you living there, renting it out, or is it sitting empty?"
  • "If we could pay cash and close on your timeline, is selling something you'd consider?"

If they're warm

"Great — here's what happens next: my partner reviews the property and puts a real cash number together for you. What's the best time for him to call you back today?" → mark Hot Lead (or Appointment Set if a time is locked).

If they say no

"Totally understand — thanks for your time." → Not Interested. If they say don't call again → DNC (that seals the number everywhere, for good).

Rules

  • Never quote a price on the call — numbers come from the partner.
  • Never claim we drove by the house.
  • Wrong person? → Wrong Number. Machine or no answer files itself.
