Booking

When a seller is ready to talk, the bot offers real open times on your Seller Calls calendar using the rule below — always concrete times inside the seller's own stated availability, never a vague "we'll reach out."

If no open time fits inside the seller's stated window, the bot hands the conversation to you instead of offering times outside it. A month a seller names as their moving timeline is a closing fact, never a call time — the bot offers near-term call times now.
