● Talk track — the framework for every call

1 · Opener

  • Say your name and why you're calling — keep it short.
  • Confirm you're talking to the owner of the property.
  • Ask if they'd consider selling if the numbers worked.

2 · Qualify

  • Do you own the home?
  • How long have you owned it?
  • Is anyone living there now — you, family, or a tenant?
  • If you sold, when would you want it done by?
  • Do you have a price in mind?
  • Is there anyone else who'd need to agree before you could sell?

3 · Hot-lead close

  • If they're open to selling: book the partner call — offer a time, confirm this is the best number, and tell them your partner will call to talk through numbers and next steps.
  • If the timing isn't right: agree on a callback time before you hang up.
  • After the call: tag the lead hot lead in the CRM so it lands on the board.
