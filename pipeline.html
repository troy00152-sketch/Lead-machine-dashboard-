Search filters the board by deal name, address, or phone. Move a deal with the "Move to…" menu on its card — it writes the new stage to HighLevel, the same as dragging the card in the CRM, and the board re-renders in place. Deals come from the dedicated cc-data getOpportunities feed, which returns every pipeline and stage from HighLevel (including empty stages), every fetched opportunity with its stage name resolved server-side, value, contact phone + contact id, pipeline, and dates. Closed deals render as rows in their Closed / Sold / Won stage columns like any other deal — no longer count + volume only.

Honest limit: HighLevel holds far more opportunities than one function call can fetch (12,917 total on Nov 27 vs a 20-page / 2,000-opp per-call cap), so the board shows the newest fetched slice, labelled above with the exact total and the per-pipeline counts. Empty stages render as empty columns, not missing ones. Contact phones are enriched for up to 200 fetched deals per load (deal pipelines first); the rest show no phone until enriched.
